This is a starter post. Keep it as a guide, or delete it once you have published your own.

## Start with the situation, not the lesson

People search for help when they are *in* a situation: "I just got laid off", "my sourdough won't rise", "my landlord is keeping my deposit". Open with the situation you were in, in the words someone would type into a search box or ask an AI assistant. That is how the right reader finds you.

## Be specific

"Network more" helps nobody. "I sent 40 short messages to former coworkers over two weeks; 11 replied, and 2 led to interviews" helps a lot. Numbers, timelines, tool names, costs and mistakes are what make experience worth reading.

## Say what you'd do differently

Hindsight is the most valuable part. If you only share what worked, readers can't tell luck from skill.

## One note, one problem

A note that solves one problem well is easier to find, tag and quote than a long post covering ten. If you have ten lessons, write ten notes.

## Let the AI do the filing

You don't need to pick perfect tags or write a summary. Run `npm run tag` and Claude reads each new note, adds topic tags, a short summary, the key takeaways and the questions it answers. Those make the note easier to find for people browsing the site and for AI assistants answering someone's question. Check what it wrote; you can always edit it by hand.
